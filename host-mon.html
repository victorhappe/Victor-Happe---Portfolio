Høst Møn Festival

Project overview

Høst Møn was a festival project focused on creating a new visual identity, design system and digital prototype for a Danish music festival.

The project was based on research, interviews, user testing and a full design process. We worked with branding, communication, content structure and user experience.

Our concept focused on creating a warm, atmospheric and nature-inspired festival identity that reflected the feeling of community, music and summer.

For my portfolio, this project shows my experience with visual identity, concept development, digital design systems and presenting a creative universe across different touchpoints.

Process and learning

We worked with the Double Diamond method, starting with research and problem exploration before moving into concept development, prototyping and testing.

Through interviews and user testing, we gained insight into how different target groups experience festival websites, ticket information, program pages and visual communication.

In the design process, we developed moodboards, typography choices, color palettes, components and layout principles. The visual style was built around warm colors, natural tones and a relaxed festival atmosphere.

One of the central parts of the project was the program page, where we experimented with interactive hover effects to make the artist lineup more engaging and visually dynamic.

This project helped me understand how branding, UX, visual design and digital storytelling can work together to create a stronger identity for an event or cultural brand.
