Dankort Campaign

Project overview

This project was developed as part of a case about increasing awareness of Dankort Øremærket among young adults aged 18–28.

The goal was to communicate how choosing Dankort can support Danish nature through Øremærket, while making the concept more visible, relatable and engaging for a younger target group.

My group developed a digital campaign concept consisting of a website, video content and social media material. The solution focused on visual storytelling, nature, transparency and simple communication.

Concept and process

We started the project by researching the target group and looking into how young adults relate to payment cards, sustainability and digital campaigns. A key insight was that the message needed to be simple, visual and easy to understand quickly.

Based on this, we created a campaign universe where Dankort Øremærket was connected to concrete nature experiences and visible impact. The website introduced the concept through a long-scroll structure, supported by sections about personal benefits, Danish nature and partner-related content.

I worked with the visual presentation of the concept, the website structure, content sections and the overall storytelling. The project also included video production and social media content, which made it especially relevant for my interest in visual communication and content creation.

This project shows my ability to combine campaign thinking, digital design, video content and storytelling into one coherent concept.
