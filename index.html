About me

Hi, I'm Victor Happe, a Multimedia Design student at KEA – Copenhagen School of Design and Technology. I am especially interested in video production, visual storytelling and digital content creation.

Through my studies, I have worked with branding, UX, web design, frontend development, content production and video editing. I enjoy combining visuals, storytelling and digital experiences to communicate ideas in a clear and engaging way.
