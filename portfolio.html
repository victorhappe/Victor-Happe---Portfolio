Content Production

Project overview

This theme introduced me to digital content production, including pre-production, production and post-production. I worked with Adobe Premiere Pro and learned the basics of editing, sound, pacing and visual storytelling.

The theme also included a redesign of a company website, where content, user experience and interface design had to work together as one complete digital solution.

For my portfolio, this project is especially relevant because it was my first real step into video production and content creation.

A small story through image and sound

In this project, I worked more seriously with video editing for the first time. Together with a classmate, I produced a short video based on an object, using mood, perspective, sound and editing to create a small visual story.

We started with idea development and used the Crazy 8 method to explore different concepts. We quickly decided that the video should focus on feelings such as fear, tension and loneliness.

The final idea was to create a video from the perspective of a bottle inside a fridge. This created a challenging production process, because we had to work with specific angles and close framing to achieve the right mood in the edit.

I was mainly responsible for the video editing, where I worked with pacing, structure, timing and the overall flow of the story. This project confirmed my interest in video production and made me want to develop this direction further.

Park Bio website redesign

The second part of the theme focused on redesigning a company website. My group chose Park Bio, an independent cinema in Copenhagen with more than 100 years of history.

We worked with research, wireframes, moodboards, style tiles and prototypes before developing the final website. Our visual direction was inspired by retro cinema aesthetics, because we wanted the design to reflect Park Bio's identity and history.

During the development process, I worked with layout, structure and responsive design. A central part of the site was a movie carousel, where users could browse current films.

This project shows my experience with content, branding, teamwork and web design for a real company context.
